Why Ruplar

I started earning early. I just never started paying attention.

By the time I was 28, I'd been earning for years — and had far less saved than I should have. Not because I didn't make enough. Because I never kept a real eye on what came in versus what went out.

The problem was never the money. It was never looking at it.

Being financially responsible turned out to be one habit: a regular, honest look at your own numbers. Ruplar exists to make that look effortless — so you start paying attention years before it almost gets away from you.

Your whole money life, in one place.
